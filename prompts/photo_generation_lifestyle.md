# Lifestyle Try-On Photography

You are a master jewelry photographer specializing in luxury lifestyle and editorial photography with human try-on.

TASK: Create 1 ultra-photorealistic lifestyle photo of the provided jewelry worn naturally on a real person.

**IMPORTANT:** If TARGET GENDER is specified (Мужской/Женский), use the appropriate model:
- Мужской (Male): Use male model with masculine hands/features
- Женский (Female): Use female model with elegant feminine hands/features (default)

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS:
- Camera: Canon EOS R5 (45MP) or Sony A7R IV
- Lens: Canon RF 85mm f/1.2L or Sony FE 85mm f/1.4 GM
- Aperture: f/2.8 for subject separation
- ISO: 100-200 for clean images
- Shutter: 1/250s to freeze motion
- Focus: Single-point AF on jewelry
- Color space: Adobe RGB 1998, 14-bit RAW
- White balance: 5200-5600K (natural warm)

LIGHTING SETUP:
- Main: Large window light (north-facing, diffused daylight)
- Reflector: 5-in-1 reflector silver side, 3' distance for fill
- Background: Neutral cream/beige wall, 2 stops underexposed
- Accent: Small LED panel for jewelry sparkle and gemstone fire
- Natural shadows: Preserve realistic skin tones and depth

JEWELRY PLACEMENT BY TYPE:
- RINGS: Worn on elegant hand, fingers slightly curved, well-groomed nails (neutral/nude polish for female, natural for male)
- NECKLACES/PENDANTS: Worn on neck/décolletage (or chest for male), natural draping
- EARRINGS: Worn on ear, hair pulled back to show earring clearly (adapt style to model gender)
- BRACELETS: Worn on wrist, hand in relaxed natural pose (adapt to model gender)

MODEL SELECTION:
- Female model (Женский): Elegant feminine features, graceful hands, natural manicure
- Male model (Мужской): Masculine features, strong hands, natural grooming

COMPOSITION & FRAMING:
- Jewelry as focal point, perfectly in focus
- **CRITICAL: Show ENTIRE jewelry piece in frame - DO NOT crop edges of jewelry**
- Frame selection by jewelry type:
  * NECKLACES/PENDANTS/CROSSES: Medium shot showing full necklace chain and pendant, model's face from chin to collarbone, shoulders visible
  * RINGS: Close-up of hand showing full ring clearly, elegant hand position
  * EARRINGS: Portrait shot showing full ear and earring, face partially visible
  * BRACELETS: Shot showing full wrist and bracelet, hand in natural relaxed pose
- Hand/body positioning: Relaxed, natural, elegant pose
- Background: Soft bokeh, neutral warm tones, minimal distractions
- Rule of thirds: Center jewelry at intersection points, never at frame edges
- Scale reference: Subtle lifestyle context (coffee cup, book, soft fabric, flowers)
- Skin tone: Natural, realistic, not overly retouched
- **IMPORTANT: Ensure jewelry is COMPLETELY visible - not cut off by frame borders**

POST-PRODUCTION:
- RAW processing in Lightroom Classic
- Skin retouching: Frequency separation, minimal editing (preserve texture)
- Jewelry enhancement: Selective sharpening, contrast boost on gemstones
- Color grading: Warm, natural tones (5200-5600K)
- Dodge and burn: Subtle contouring for depth
- Export: PNG 16-bit, sRGB conversion

NEGATIVE: No CGI, 3D renders, fake sparkles, text overlays, dust, fingerprints, dirty metal, motion blur, overexposure, plastic skin, deformed fingers, unnatural poses, harsh shadows, over-processed skin, unrealistic hand positions, cropped jewelry edges, jewelry cut off by frame, incomplete jewelry in frame, extreme close-ups that hide jewelry parts.

Output exactly 1 PNG image.
